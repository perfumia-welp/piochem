Create a premium, high-conversion B2B corporate homepage concept for:

# FREE TRADE EGYPT

Chemical, Raw Material & Ingredient Supplier
Egypt

This is a **concept redesign / demo homepage** intended to show how Free Trade Egypt could reposition itself digitally to attract international buyers, procurement managers, manufacturers, distributors and business partners outside Egypt.

The website should NOT feel like:

* A basic local company website
* A traditional chemical catalogue
* A retail e-commerce store
* A generic corporate template

Instead, it should feel like a modern international company with strong commercial credibility, technical competence and export capability.

---

# CORE BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The website should convert international visitors into:

* Request for Quotation inquiries
* Product sourcing inquiries
* Long-term B2B supply opportunities
* Distributor / partnership opportunities
* Technical documentation requests
* Direct conversations with the sales team

The primary conversion CTA throughout the website is:

**Request a Quote**

Secondary CTAs can include:

**Explore Products**
**Discuss Your Requirements**
**Request Technical Documents**

---

# DESIGN INSPIRATION

The design should combine ideas from several world-class digital experiences without copying any one website directly.

### Stripe

Use the clarity, strong hierarchy, confidence and conversion-focused storytelling.

The page should quickly establish:

* What Free Trade does
* Why the company is credible
* What industries it serves
* Why customers should work with it
* How to contact the company

### Linear

Use the premium visual feeling:

* Elegant typography
* Generous spacing
* Sophisticated dark sections
* Controlled gradients
* Minimal visual noise
* Subtle motion
* High-end digital presentation

### Vercel

Use:

* Precise grid layouts
* Technical information blocks
* Refined cards
* Strong black / white contrast
* Clean diagrams
* Documentation-oriented UI
* Modern engineering feel

### Azelis

Use the B2B chemical-industry logic:

* Markets / industries
* Product categories
* Technical expertise
* Quality
* Documentation
* Sourcing
* Supply chain
* Product inquiry
* International business positioning

### Houd El Nile

Use the export-oriented storytelling approach:

* Strong company positioning

# FINAL EXPERIENCE

When an international procurement manager lands on this website, the desired reaction is:

**“This company looks established, professional and capable. I can send them my specification and ask for a quotation.”**

The final design should feel comparable in quality to a modern European or international B2B company website while remaining appropriate and believable for the chemical distribution industry.

Prioritize:

**Trust + Capability + International Positioning + Conversion**

over:

**Decoration + excessive product listings + generic corporate content.**
