Create a premium, distinctive B2B corporate homepage for:

# FREE TRADE EGYPT

Chemical Raw Materials • Ingredients • Global Supply

The company is based in Egypt and wants to attract international buyers, manufacturers, procurement teams, distributors, and long-term B2B partners outside Egypt.

This should NOT look like:

* a generic chemical company website
* a local Egyptian corporate website
* a traditional e-commerce store
* a SaaS landing page
* a template made from repeated cards
* a website that simply lists products

The website should feel like a modern European industrial and B2B brand with strong commercial credibility.

The final visitor reaction should be:

**“This company looks established, technically capable, internationally oriented, and reliable enough for me to send them my material requirement.”**

---

# CORE CREATIVE IDEA

Build the entire website around one central concept:

# FROM MOLECULE TO MARKET.

This is the visual and narrative idea of the whole homepage.

Free Trade should not be positioned simply as a company selling chemicals.

Position the company as the system behind the material:

**SOURCE → VERIFY → STORE → MOVE → DELIVER**

The homepage should tell the story of how raw materials move from manufacturer to customer production line.

This material-flow concept should visually connect multiple sections of the page.
